SYSTEM:
You generate open-domain QA pairs that require CROSS-COMMUNITY SENSEMAKING over the provided STORY ARTIFACTS.

What you see (and ONLY what you can use):
- COMMUNITY_CARDS: community summaries + grounded findings (with IDs).
- CONNECTOR_RELATIONS: explicit cross-community relations (with IDs) linking communities.

Definition:
A cross-community sensemaking question cannot be answered using a single community card alone (or one isolated finding).
It MUST require integrating information from MULTIPLE communities AND at least ONE explicit connector relation that links those communities.

Required constraints (must satisfy for every item):
1) Multi-community requirement:
   - Each QA MUST require at least TWO distinct communities.
   - supporting_findings MUST include ≥1 finding from EACH required community.
2) Connector requirement:
   - Each QA MUST require ≥1 connector relation.
   - The cited connector(s) MUST be necessary: if the cited connectors were removed, the question becomes unanswerable or ambiguous.
   - The cited connector(s) MUST link the required communities (directly or via the stated relation).
3) Objective-answer requirement:
   - The question MUST have a single best answer supported by the provided findings/connectors.
   - Do NOT generate subjective, interpretive, or perspective-dependent questions or answers.

Question style (ONE sentence, realistic, user-like):
- Sound like a real user trying to understand a situation (not a quiz).
- Prefer grounded sensemaking intents that hinge on explicit cross-community links:
  connection, trigger→response, cause→consequence, mechanism/role, stated condition/constraint.
- Good patterns include:
  "How is X connected to Y?",
  "What action/decision led to Y?",
  "What triggered Y after X?",
  "How did X contribute to Y?",
  "What stated condition/demand tied X and Y together?"
- Do NOT mention graphs, hypergraphs, communities, summaries, findings, packets, datasets, or “according to the graph.”
- Avoid exam/trivia tone (e.g., “Which leader…”, “What country…”, “In what year…”) unless it still clearly requires multi-community + connector dependence.

Do NOT generate:
- Subjective/interpretive prompts: “significance,” “importance,” “what it reveals,” “how it reflects,” “was it justified,” “who was right,” “how people felt,” “what it meant.”
- Anything whose answer could vary by perspective, framing, or values.
- Pure lookup / identity-only / alias-only questions (e.g., “Who is X?”, “What is X’s real name?”),
  unless the question ALSO requires a non-identity connector and genuine cross-community integration.
- Broad overview questions (“What happened?”, “Summarize…”) that do not force a specific connector-dependent cross-community link.

Answer style:
- Short, factual, uniquely supported by the artifacts (aim ≤ 12 tokens; slightly longer only if unavoidable).
- No hedging (“might/could/likely”) and no normative language (“good/bad/important”).
- Do NOT introduce facts not present in the provided findings/connectors.
- If a unique objective answer is not supported, do not output that QA pair.

Intent labels:
Set intent_pattern to one of ["explainer","connection","trigger","consequence","stake"] where:
- explainer: a grounded explanation that hinges on a specific cross-community link (not “significance”)
- connection: how two developments/entities are related via connector(s)
- trigger: what action/decision/event led to a cross-community response/outcome
- consequence: what outcome followed from a cross-community link
- stake: a concrete stated constraint/condition/demand/objective tying communities (NOT “why was it important”)

Internal self-checks (run these before outputting each item; do not output the check text):
A) Objective-answer test:
   - Is there exactly one best short answer supported by the cited findings/connectors?
   - Would reasonable readers give the same answer?
B) Multi-community necessity test:
   - If ANY one required community card is removed, does the question become unanswerable or ambiguous?
C) Connector necessity test:
   - If ALL cited connector relations are removed, does the question become unanswerable or ambiguous?
D) Anti-trivia test:
   - Does it feel like a real sensemaking question (not a quiz or entity-attribute lookup)?

Output:
Return a JSON array of up to {K} items. Each item must be:
- question: string (ONE sentence)
- answer: string (short, objective, supported)
- required_communities: [2+ community_id]
- supporting_findings: [{community_id: ..., finding_id: ...}, ...]  (≥1 per required community)
- supporting_connectors: [connector_id, ...] (≥1; must be necessary; must link required communities)
- intent_pattern: one of ["explainer","connection","trigger","consequence","stake"]
- why_multi_community: ONE sentence explaining why multiple communities + connector(s) are required
  (describe it in content terms; do NOT mention graphs/communities/packets)

Diversity:
Across the set, vary intent_pattern when possible and avoid using the same connector_id for all items unless unavoidable.
